Privacy Policy

What NetNest stores, where it lives, and what we do not collect.

Effective November 13, 2026 · Version 1.1

DNS history stays in your house. The box records lookups on its own storage. We do not sell that history, and we do not upload it to operate the service.

1. Who we are

VibeDevs3 (“NetNest”, “we”) makes the NetNest parent app, the NetNest Kids companion, and the NetNest One appliance. This policy covers those products.

2. What stays on the box

The NetNest One in your home stores:

  • DNS activity. Domains devices on your Wi-Fi ask for, and whether the box blocked them. The box keeps this for about 30 days, then deletes it.
  • Devices. Names you set, and the local IP and MAC address the box sees.
  • Children. Profile name, avatar, rules, and bedtime schedules that you create. Children do not get their own NetNest accounts.
  • Box passcode. Stored only as a hash on the box. We cannot read it.

After the box decides a lookup, it may ask Quad9 for the normal answer. That is a DNS resolver on the internet. The decision and the log stay on the box.

3. What the phone stores

The parent app keeps a copy of profiles, devices, and recent activity so the screens can open on your home Wi-Fi and show the last copy when you are away. Signing out of the parent account clears that local copy. The box passcode session is separate and ends when you disconnect the box in the app.

4. What the parent account stores

Sign-in uses Google Firebase. We store the least the account needs:

  • Email address, display name, and the account id from Firebase.
  • A sign-in session on your phone.

We do not put your household DNS history in that account. Google handles Google Sign-In under its own terms.

5. Children

NetNest is for parents and guardians. We do not ask a child to create an account or to give us an email address. A child profile is a name and settings you enter, stored on the box. The NetNest Kids app on a child’s phone uses a PIN you set. It is not a separate cloud account for the child.

6. How we use this

  • To sign you in and to reach your box on the home network.
  • To apply the rules and schedules you set.
  • To answer a support email you send us.

We do not sell personal information or DNS history.

7. Security

  • The box passcode is hashed with bcrypt.
  • Parent sign-in uses Firebase over HTTPS.
  • The phone talks to the box on your home network. That path is local. Protect the Wi-Fi password and the box passcode.

8. Your choices

  • Sign out, or ask us to delete the parent account, by emailing privacy@netnest.app.
  • Factory-reset the box in the app to erase local profiles, devices, rules, and activity.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes. The app links to this page.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@netnest.app. Support: support@netnest.app.
